This folder is empty on purpose.

The hero video and the dish photos live on the Higgsfield CDN, and the build
environment that produced this site could not download them (its network policy
blocks that host), so they could not be bundled into the archive.

index.html already points at the CDN, so it works as-is when you open it.

To make this folder self-contained, run from the parent directory:

    ./fetch-assets.sh

That downloads all ten files here, compresses them if ffmpeg / ImageMagick are
installed, and switches index.html over to ./assets/.

Files it will fetch:
    hero.mp4              15s, 1284x716, the tea leaf salad turning in 3D
    tea-leaf-salad.png    also used as the video poster and the 3D turntable
    samusa-soup.png
    rainbow-salad.png
    platha.png
    sesame-beef.png
    garlic-noodles.png
    pork-belly.png
    coconut-rice.png
    chili-lamb.png
